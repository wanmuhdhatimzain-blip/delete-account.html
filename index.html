Account & Data Deletion Request

If you wish to delete your account and all associated data stored with My Spending Note, you can do so through either of the options below:

Option 1: In-App Deletion (Recommended)

1. Open the My Spending Note app.
2. Navigate to Settings.
3. Tap Delete Account and confirm.

This immediately and permanently deletes your account and all synced spending records from our Firebase servers.

Option 2: Email Deletion Request

If you have uninstalled the app or cannot access it, email us at:
onemuhdhatimzayn@gmail.com

Subject: Account Deletion Request - My Spending Note
Details to include: The registered email address associated with your account.

We will delete your Firebase authentication account and associated records within 30 days of receiving your email.
